# How to Report a Bug in Armies of Gielinor

If you experience an unexpected freeze, dark screen, premature turn end, or error while playing Armies of Gielinor, you can generate an instant diagnostic report with **one click or one keystroke**.

---

## 1. Quick Diagnostic Shortcuts

### Option A: While Inside the Game (Press `F9`)
* Tap **`F9`** on your keyboard at any point during gameplay.
* The game will chime and automatically:
  1. Capture recent game events.
  2. Clean out any private computer names or file paths.
  3. Save a report to your project's `reports/` folder.
  4. Copy the entire report to your clipboard so you can paste it immediately.
  5. Open the report in Notepad.

### Option B: Outside the Game or if Frozen (`Ctrl + Alt + B` or Desktop Shortcut)
* If the game has already closed, or if the screen is frozen:
  - Press **`Ctrl + Alt + B`** on your keyboard, OR
  - Double-click the **`Export Bug Report`** shortcut on your Windows Desktop (or in `tools/`).
* A fresh diagnostic report will open in Notepad and copy to your clipboard right away.

---

## 2. The 4 Simple Questions

At the top of your report file (or when messaging the development team), jot down quick answers to these 4 simple questions:

1. **Game Mode**: Were you in Singleplayer Campaign (which mission?) or Multiplayer (Host or Guest)?
2. **Last Action**: What did you do right before the issue? *(e.g. "Moved my Knight across 3 tiles and attacked an enemy Chieftain")*
3. **What Happened**: What did you see on your screen? *(e.g. "The attack animation finished, but the opponent's turn started immediately without letting me finish my turn")*
4. **Log Data**: Paste the text generated by the diagnostic shortcut.

---

## 3. Privacy Guarantee

The automated bug reporter strictly scrubs all personal computer names, home directories, and private identifiers before generating the report. No personal computer data is ever included in the export.
