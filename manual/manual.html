I. The God Wars of Gielinor

IN THE THIRD AGE OF GIELINOR, the cataclysmic conflict known as the God Wars engulfed every continent, forest, and desert in relentless divine warfare. The fragile balance preserved by Guthix was shattered as rival gods led legion after legion through mystical stone portals to conquer territory, capture vital settlements, and harvest the ancient Mana embedded within the soil.

As a supreme Commander chosen by fate, you are entrusted with the banners of war. By rallying local barbarian tribes, aligning with divine pantheons, and capturing strategic structures, you must direct mortal and immortal soldiers alike across rugged battlegrounds to secure total dominion.

Victory demands not only sharp steel and devastating sorcery, but cold, deliberate tactical calculation. Each turn represents a vital stroke of military maneuvering where positioning, terrain, and attrition dictate who rises as conqueror and who is banished to the abyss.

II. System Operations & Steam Multiplayer

Requirements & Native Runtime

The Standalone Edition includes an embedded OpenJDK 18 runtime environment (18.0.2.1). No separate Java installation or system PATH configuration is required. Simply launch ArmiesOfGielinor.exe on 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11.

Display Scaling & Graphics

The client rasterizer renders high-resolution 1:1 pixel assets with automatic high-DPI awareness, preventing blurry scaling on 1440p and 4K monitors. You may freely toggle between Borderless Fullscreen, Resizable Windowed, and Classic Fixed (640×480) viewports via the in-game options menu.

Hosting & Joining Steam P2P Matches

Multiplayer networking is powered by Steamworks P2P datagram relays (AppID 480). Matches are private, secure, and require no router port forwarding:

  • Hosting a Match: Launch the game, select Host, and navigate from the Main Menu to Custom Game → Create Room. Press F2 or open the Steam Overlay (Shift + Tab) to invite friends.
  • Friend Invitations: Friends receive standard Steam chat notifications with a direct Join Game prompt.

HUD Elements & Control Matrix

Camera & Navigation Controls

  • Pan Map: Hold down the Right Mouse Button and drag, or use the Arrow Keys.
  • Select Unit: Left-click directly upon the unit's circular hexagonal base tile.
  • Movement & Attack Preview: Hover over any tile to preview the fastest path. Green highlighted tiles depict reachable movement squares; red tiles and battle swords indicate enemies within attack range.
  • Cycle Units: Left-click the upper globe button to cycle focus between unmoved units and active portals.
  • End Turn: Click the flashing END TURN button once your maneuvers are completed.

IV. The Divine Pantheons

In every battle, commanders select three patron deities to support their campaign. As you recruit units aligned with a particular god, your divine favour with that deity rises, unlocking progressively more powerful holy champions and high-tier neutral mercenaries:

Saradomin (Good & Order)

Righteous god of light and wisdom. Specializes in divine armor, healing miracles, and resilient defense. Signature units: Paladins, White Knights, Monks, Centaurs, and the holy Icyene.

Zamorak (Chaos & Wrath)

Mahjarrat god of discord. Prizes offensive ferocity, dark necromancy, poison, and demonic power. Signature units: Werewolves, Skeletons, Necromancers, Black Knights, and Greater Demons.

Guthix (Balance & Nature)

Primordial balancer of Gielinor. Manipulates natural mana, commands wild beasts, and gains amplified defense in forests. Signature units: Druids, Moss Giants, Terrorbirds, and ancient Ents.

Seren (Crystal & Purity)

Crystalline patron goddess of elfkind. Leverages precision archery, nimble skirmish movement, and prism shapeshifting. Signature units: Elven Archers, Shieldmaidens, Elf Lords, and Shapeshifters.

Bandos (War & Devastation)

Big High War God of goblins, orks, and ogres. Overwhelms rivals through sheer physical strength and war beasts. Signature units: Goblins, Ogres, Orks, and colossal Ourg chieftains.

Menaphite Pantheon (Desert)

Ancient gods Tumeken, Elidinis, and Icthlarin. Masters of desert attrition, mummy resurrection, and insect swarms. Signature units: Mummies, Jackals, Nomads, and the Kalphite Queen.

VI. Tactical Combat & Terrain Rules

Combat is engaged when an attacking unit moves adjacent to an enemy (or within ranged strike distance) and issues an attack command. Battle resolution calculates offensive strength against defensive resilience using weighted dice rolls:

  • Melee Engagements: The attacker rolls offensive dice based on their Strength rating. If the defender survives, they immediately execute a Counter-Strike using their defensive capability.
  • Ranged & Siege Volleys: Archers, spearwomen, and catapults attack from multiple tiles away (e.g. range 2â€“3 or 3â€“5). Defenders cannot counter-attack against ranged strikes unless they also possess adequate counter-range.
  • Healing & Field Medicine: Units that neither move nor attack during a round regain 1 HP at the start of the next turn (Resting). Priests, Monks, and Druids can heal adjacent allies by up to 3 HP per turn and neutralize toxic poisons.
  • Undead Decay: Skeletons, Mummies, and actively poisoned units lose 1 HP at the beginning of each turn. Skeletons and mummies can only stop their decay by occupying friendly portals.

VIII. Match Victory & Defeat Rules

Multiplayer and skirmish battles in Armies of Gielinor are evaluated continuously by the authoritative battle engine. A match terminates and triggers the official Victory or Defeat declaration under the following four conditions:

  • 1. Total Portal Loss (Conquest & Barbarian Denial):
    Defined in up.java. If an opposing player loses control of all their portals, and simultaneously has zero surviving barbarians or barbarian chieftains on the battlefield, they are unable to ever capture or spawn units again. The server authoritatively declares them eliminated.
  • 2. Total Military Extinction:
    Defined in mc.java. If every single unit belonging to an army is slain in combat, that faction suffers total unit loss and is immediately vanquished from the war.
  • 3. Victory Point Domination:
    Controlling captured settlements, towers, and temples generates Victory Points at the end of every round. The first commander whose victory point gauge reaches the target match ceiling (e.g. 500, 1000, or 2000 VP) immediately secures decisive match victory.
  • 4. Turn Limit Expiration:
    When a battle reaches its preset maximum turn duration (e.g. 50 turns), sudden death evaluation occurs. The commander possessing the greatest cumulative score and structure tally is crowned the victor.
